## Calibration layer (hybrid route) — executed

Ran on lab-a40 (all 1066 checkpoints live there, not on 3090). Scripts:
`scripts/cal/{make_dev_preds,calibrate,evaluate,print_table,diag_*}.py`.
Full numbers: `paper/CALIBRATION_LAYER_RESULTS.md`, JSON in `experiments/cal/eval_{A,BE,PE}/`.

- Layer: 66-param head, Student-t over 126 bins, right-censored NLL, fitted on pooled
  out-of-fold dev predictions (709 engines, 4 fleets). Scale chosen on 5-fold
  cross-fitted dev, smallest scale reaching nominal 90% coverage.
- Result across 12 backbone×fleet instances: CRPS -6..-14%, Winkler90 -3..-20%,
  MPIW90 -7..-19%, PICP90 0.89-0.96. Point accuracy shifts <=0.2 cycles.
- Protocol ablation: per-subset fails on FD003 (0.77); LOSO over-conservative
  (0.96-0.97); pooled is the only stable protocol. Single-fleet dev cannot certify
  coverage — cross-fit reports 0.88 while test gives 0.75.
- Capped-label convention induces a -8.5..-12.1 bias in the [100,126) band on all four
  fleets (32-60% of that band is censored). Aggregate coverage hides it.
- Rejected: mean-preserving calibration (exponential tilt) — coverage 0.85, CRPS worse;
  the learned E[Y|yhat] != yhat is real information. Global-argmin scale selection —
  coverage is non-monotone in scale (uniform limit excludes the cap bin).
- Cost: A40 is shared and was at load 49; pin OMP_NUM_THREADS=4, one arm takes 21 s.

## Decision state

Hybrid route delivered a defensible measurement/calibration contribution, not an
architecture contribution. Backbone untouched at 10.45/11.96/10.09/12.27. Open question
