## The ledger is engine-mean, uncapped

`a8_engine_level.csv::val_eng` reproduces from `val_pred.csv` as the
**uncapped** engine-mean to 5.3e-15, and the capped reading misses it by up to 0.2172.
So the ledger is exact and its convention is not the frozen one: the
predictions in these runs go above 125 (the CSV label is already capped,
the prediction is not), and clipping them changes the engine-mean RMSE.
**The two readings are not interchangeable**: a module ranked on one
can move on the other, so any table that mixes the ledger with a capped
recomputation is comparing two protocols.

## The A8 main effects under both conventions

The 96-cell ledger is the uncapped reading, so the reported main
effects are uncapped-protocol effects. Recomputing the same 2^3
design under the frozen cap gives:

| effect | uncapped (the ledger) | capped (frozen protocol) | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| ms | -0.0818 | -0.0892 | -0.0074 |
| sa | -0.1625 | -0.1637 | -0.0011 |
| hi | -0.0495 | -0.0486 | +0.0010 |

The signs and the order of the three effects are the same under both
readings; the magnitudes move by at most 0.007. So the attribution
conclusions do not turn on the cap, but the numbers quoted must say
which convention produced them.
