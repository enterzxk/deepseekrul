results: pre-registered tail guard makes the A gate pass on both fleets

The observation-span schedule reduced RMSE on both fleets but failed the asymmetric-score
clause on FD004: one seed and fold produced a 96-cycle over-estimate on a single engine,
which the exponential penalty turned into a fleet mean of 313 against 23 for the reference.

Rule pre-registered in PROTOCOL 8.8 before any guarded result was computed: when the two
models disagree by more than tau = 50 cycles, keep the short-window prediction. Sensitivity
reported for tau in {30, 50, 75}.

The guard also repairs the M0 arm on FD004 (-2.03% with an interval crossing zero becomes
-4.39% with [-0.851, -0.348]).

Negative result recorded in PROTOCOL 8.7: adding modules does not work at this data scale.
Multi-scale convolution + extra transformer layer + attention pooling, at the baseline width
(116k params), costs 7.4%; with extra width 21%; with stronger regularisation 42%. The FULL
arm degrades from 12.751 to 15.928. Capacity is not the bottleneck for 41k training windows.

Disclosures kept in the package: the unguarded FD004 result (NASA 37.76), the single-cell
regression on seed 202 FD004 (8.63 to 9.71), and the fact that the guard was motivated by an
observed failure and only then pre-registered.
