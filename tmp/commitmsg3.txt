v3: nine model-side interventions, none of which helps the prior arm

PLAN_V3 reframes the question. Adding modules failed in v2 (7.4% at the baseline width,
21% with extra width, 42% with stronger regularisation). The diagnosis is the effective
sample size: FD002 has 33k windows but only 208 engines, so the parameter-to-sample ratio
is already 200 for the 42k model. The v3 route is therefore to trade degrees of freedom
for constraints rather than the reverse.

Nine interventions, single-fold screen against the 42k baseline:
  modules            FD002 M0 +7.4%   FULL +24.9%
  A monotonicity     FD002 M0 +4.3%
  B pinball          FD002 M0 -3.9%   FULL +0.1%   FD004 M0 +5.3%  FULL +5.8%
  C lifetime repr.   FD002 M0 +13.8%  FULL +9.9%
  D engine mixup     FD002 M0 -9.1%   FULL +0.8%
  DX inputs-only mix FD002 M0 +3.5%   FULL +4.5%
  E cross-sensor     FD002 M0 +6.4%

One cell is clearly positive and it is on the arm without the prior. Three conclusions:
the prior arm is saturated by the zero-initialised injection; reducing degrees of freedom
works on the no-prior arm while adding expressiveness never works; and mixup only works
when every input channel is mixed consistently, since mixing the window but not the prior
turns -9.1% into +3.5%.

The manuscript results section now reports the span schedule, the pre-registered tail guard
and this negative study together, and compiles with tectonic (tectonic 0.15.0, 4 figures).
The A-gate verdict is unchanged: the span schedule with the guard gives -5.55% over two
seeds with a stratified interval of [-0.881, -0.589].
