
\subsection{Scope}

The two single-condition fleets behave differently. Mixup on the plain backbone is 1.04 percent worse than the prior arm on FD001 (10.341 against 10.235) and 4.67 percent worse on FD003 (10.632 against 10.158), although on FD003 it is still 2.13 percent better than the plain backbone itself. On the multi-condition fleets the same recipe is 10.00 and 9.44 percent better than the prior arm.

The reading we favour is that the prior carries information that nothing else supplies when the operating condition is fixed, whereas under six conditions the mixed-condition regularisation is worth more than the prior. We therefore do not claim that the recipe is uniformly better; it trades a small loss on single-condition fleets for a large gain on multi-condition ones, and it does so without a lifetime prior.
